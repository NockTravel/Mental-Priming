Quiet Eye

Hold the gaze

A long, stable final fixation on the aim point is one of the strongest predictors of accuracy in aiming sports — and it's trainable.

How it works

1Press and hold on the gold dot, and keep your eyes locked on it.
2Keep your finger still — drifting or lifting early breaks the hold.
3Red flashes will try to pull your eye. Ignore them. Each rep holds a little longer.
